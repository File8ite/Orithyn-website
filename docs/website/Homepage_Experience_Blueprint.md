# Orythyn Homepage Experience Blueprint

> **Project:** Orythyn Corporate Website
> **Page:** Homepage
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** UX & Content Planning

---

# Page Purpose

The homepage is the digital front door to Orythyn.

Its primary objective is to establish credibility, communicate value, and encourage qualified visitors to start a conversation.

The page should answer the following questions in sequence:

1. Who is Orythyn?
2. What does Orythyn do?
3. Why should I trust Orythyn?
4. Can Orythyn solve my problem?
5. What should I do next?

---

# User Intent

## Primary Visitors

- Business owners
- Startup founders
- School administrators
- NGO leaders
- Operations managers

## Visitor Goals

- Find a reliable software engineering partner.
- Understand available services.
- Evaluate credibility.
- Decide whether to get in touch.

# Section 1 — Hero

## Objective

Communicate Orythyn's value proposition within the first few seconds.

## User Question

"What does this company do?"

## Key Message

Orythyn builds reliable software that helps organizations operate more efficiently, serve customers better, and grow with confidence.

## Content

### Headline

A clear, benefit-focused statement.

### Supporting Copy

A short paragraph explaining who Orythyn serves and the outcomes clients can expect.

### Primary CTA

Book a Discovery Call

### Secondary CTA

Explore Our Services

### Visual Direction

Modern product illustration or abstract engineering-themed visual.

### SEO Focus

Software Engineering Company

Custom Software Development

# Success Criteria

A successful homepage should enable a visitor to:

- Understand what Orythyn does within 10 seconds.
- Identify whether Orythyn can solve their problem.
- Trust Orythyn's professionalism.
- Know the next step without hesitation.
- Complete a contact action with minimal friction.
